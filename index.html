Smart Parameter Extraction
Automatically reads complete blood counts (CBC), lipid profiles, glucose, kidney, liver, thyroid, and urinary parameters from text-based PDFs or image scans.

Age & Sex-Specific Reference Ranges
Evaluates observed numeric values against reference boundaries matched to your profile age and sex at birth, flagging normal, borderline, and high/low values.

Historical Trend Analysis
Securely saves reports to your local timeline to automatically chart biological changes in biomarkers over months and years, helping you track your wellness.
